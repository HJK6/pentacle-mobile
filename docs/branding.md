# Pentacle branding

The supplied 1024×1024 logo is retained at `assets/images/pentacle_icon_source.png`. Its SHA256 is
`6f6533d7585b3532e63ec8226b218e3c47658d212734e3c9ff181f8be7c35117`.
The launcher derivative translates the artwork 2 pixels right and 29 pixels down
on the same 1024-pixel canvas to center its circle, extending the original black
edge into the exposed margin. The original master stays unchanged. Derive required
sizes from the centered image with Lanczos filtering; preserve artwork and colors.

Expo uses the logo for the 1024-pixel app and splash icons and 256-pixel
favicon. Android adaptive foreground insets the centered square to two thirds
on its original corner color; `app.config.ts` wires that asset explicitly.
Native icon slots are generated by Expo prebuild from these sources. The existing
notification small-icon consumer retains its platform tint behavior.

The public assistant display fallback is Zephyr. Explicit daemon display names
retain precedence; existing composite addresses, routes and database keys are
protocol identifiers and stay compatible.
